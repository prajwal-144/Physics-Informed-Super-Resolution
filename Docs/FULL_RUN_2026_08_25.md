# Full run from scratch: 3 classes, 10,000 train / 2,000 val

Every command is run from the **`Grid_Based_Experiment` root**, never from inside `superres/`.
Nothing below says "same as above". Copy and paste in order.

---

## Step 0. Prepare the folders

```
ren superres\results old_results
mkdir superres\results
```

`old_results/` keeps everything from the previous run. Do not delete it: the previous B4 and B5
checkpoints are the only copies of results the current draft quotes, and you will want them if this
run has to be abandoned.

**Every default path in every script still points at `superres/results/`,** which is now the new
empty folder, so defaults resolve to the new run. Every command below passes its paths explicitly
anyway.

## Time budget

| steps | what | rough wall clock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | gates, response, ladder | 10 min |
| 5 to 8 | Path A and its scoring | 1 h |
| 9 to 12 | two B3 runs and their evaluation | 4 to 6 h |
| 13 | refinement on 2,000 val | 40 min |
| 14 | 10,000 train lenses | 4 h |
| 15 to 18 | two B4 runs and figures | 3 h |
| 19 | injection | 10 min |
| 20 | B5 gate | 1.5 h |
| 21 | two extra seeds | 3 h |

Roughly 15 hours without step 21, 18 with. Steps 9, 10, 14, 15, 16 and 20 are the long ones and
should run overnight.

## Abort points

If you run out of time, stop at a step boundary and keep what you have. The paper degrades
gracefully in this order:

- **After step 8** you have the whole of Section 2, Table 1's last row, Appendix A, B, C and D, and
  the three-class claim. That is already a submittable paper with the old B3/B4/B5 numbers quoted
  from `old_results/` and labelled axion-only.
- **After step 13** you additionally have Section 5 in full.
- **After step 18** you have Table 1 complete and Section 4's licence paragraph.
- **After step 20** you have everything the current draft claims.

## Two things that will save you an hour each

Step 14 is the single longest leg and it depends only on step 9. Launch step 9 first, and while B3
trains you can do steps 4, 7 and 8, which touch nothing B3 needs.

If any step fails with an `index/path mismatch` error, that is the new guard in `data_a.py` doing its
job: a JSON from `old_results/` has been passed to a script running under the new ordering. Fix the
path in the command rather than disabling the check.
